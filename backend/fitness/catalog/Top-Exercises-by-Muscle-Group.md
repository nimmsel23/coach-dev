# Top Exercises by Muscle Group

A practical strength & hypertrophy exercise knowledge base.

---

# Chest (Brust)

## S-Tier Compound Movements

### Barbell Bench Press
**Primary Muscles**
- Pectoralis major
- Anterior deltoid
- Triceps brachii

**Why**
- maximal overload potential
- classic strength benchmark
- excellent compound hypertrophy tool

**Variations**
- flat bench
- paused bench
- close grip bench
- reverse grip bench
- Larsen press

---

### Incline Dumbbell Press
**Primary Muscles**
- upper chest
- anterior delts
- triceps

**Why**
- excellent upper chest bias
- greater ROM than barbell
- more natural shoulder path
- better unilateral balance demands

**Variations**
- low incline
- high incline
- neutral grip
- alternating

---

### Dips
**Primary Muscles**
- lower chest
- triceps
- anterior delts

**Why**
- incredible stretch loading
- strong overload potential
- bodyweight or weighted

**Variations**
- chest dips
- upright triceps dips
- weighted dips
- ring dips

---

## Isolation / Hypertrophy

### Cable Fly
**Bias**
depends on angle

- low to high → upper chest
- midline → full chest
- high to low → lower chest

---

### Pec Deck
**Why**
- stable setup
- easy progression
- strong contraction
- low technical demand

---

### Machine Chest Press
**Why**
- great stimulus to fatigue ratio
- safer near failure
- highly effective hypertrophy tool
